Leadership

An operating rhythm built for decisions, not status updates.

From a baseline of zero OKR alignment, I designed and installed a company operating system that connects strategy to weekly execution.

  1. Strategy — a ten-pillar operating planSequenced by dependency and tiered by priority, spanning AI transformation, revenue, governance, talent, culture and finance.
  2. Ownership — named directly responsible individualsEvery pillar has one accountable owner, removing ambiguity and single points of failure.
  3. Objectives — cascading key resultsCompany goals cascade into team OKRs, closing the gap between plan and execution.
  4. Cadence — a weekly decision forumLeadership time spent on decisions, backed by written pipeline updates and bi-weekly product stability reviews.
  5. Transparency — published scorecardsMonthly all-hands with an unsanitised performance scorecard, plus cash and runway visibility.
